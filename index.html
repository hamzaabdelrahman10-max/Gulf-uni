        Project Overview

        Investigating how AI transforms medical diagnosis through advanced image analysis

        Our Focus

        This project explores how Artificial Intelligence analyzes medical images — including X-rays, CT scans, and MRIs — to identify early signs of disease that may escape human observation.

        Why Early Detection Matters

        Detecting conditions at their earliest stages dramatically improves treatment outcomes, reduces complications, and lowers global mortality rates.

        Comprehensive Analysis

        We compare AI accuracy against human diagnosis, examine ethical frameworks, and address cybersecurity for patient data protection.

        Ethics, Advantages & Cybersecurity

        Responsible innovation in patient care

        Advantages

        • Earlier fracture detection & intervention
        • Instant first aid guidance anywhere
        • Reduced workload for medical professionals
        • Access to diagnostics in underserved regions
        • Support tool — doctors remain central

        Limitations & Ethics

        • Demo model — not for real medical use
        • Limited training dataset (3 cases)
        • Not a substitute for professional care
        • Need transparency in AI decisions
        • Requirement for continuous validation

        Cybersecurity

        • All processing happens locally in browser
        • Images never leave your device
        • No cloud API — maximum privacy
        • Compliant with data protection principles
        • Protected data = trusted AI healthcare
