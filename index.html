Introduction & Problem Statement

Why we built MedGuard AI

Background

  • Artificial Intelligence is becoming vital in medical image analysis
  • AI can identify patterns that may escape human observation
  • But AI predictions are not always reliable — especially in complex cases
  • Current systems often lack transparency and long-term patient tracking

The Problem

  • AI can produce incorrect predictions
  • Systems rarely communicate how uncertain they are
  • Previous exams may be inaccessible during follow-ups
  • Comparing images over time is difficult without organized records
  • Patient data security and privacy are critical concerns

Project Aim

  • Analyze X-rays with AI
  • Provide confidence scores for reliability
  • Flag uncertain cases for professional review
  • Store patient records & previous images securely
  • Compare exams to visualize changes over time

Our System — How It Works

Four core components

A. AI Image Analysis

  • Analyzes uploaded X-ray images
  • Identifies patterns indicating medical conditions
  • Generates AI-based predictions
  • Initially focused on fracture detection

B. Confidence Assessment

  • Provides a confidence score with every prediction
  • Estimates reliability based on testing
  • Flags low-confidence cases for doctor review
  • Never presents uncertain results as fact

C. Patient Records

  • Digital medical records for each patient
  • Unique ID system — no facial recognition
  • Stores images, predictions, dates & scores
  • Connects new exams to existing history

D. Progress Tracking

  • Compares current X-ray with previous
  • Highlights visible differences
  • Shows changes across examination dates
  • Helps doctors visualize recovery progress

What Makes Us Different

  • Uncertainty-Aware AI — Admits when it may not be sure
  • Explainable AI — Highlights which image areas influenced results
  • Longitudinal Monitoring — Tracks changes across time
  • Responsible AI — Supports doctors, never replaces them

        Data Privacy & Research Methodology

        Security, ethics, and testing standards

        Data Protection

        • Unique patient IDs — no facial recognition
        • Records encrypted at rest
        • Access restricted to authorized staff
        • Images de-identified for research
        • Exploring Federated Learning for training
        • Patient confidentiality is priority

        Research Methodology

        • Focus: X-ray fracture detection
        • Public de-identified datasets used
        • Separate training & test data
        • Evaluating: Accuracy, Sensitivity, Specificity
        • Tests uncertain/unfamiliar images
        • Demonstrates record retrieval & comparison

        Responsible Use

        • AI supports, never replaces, doctors
        • Uncertainty is flagged, not hidden
        • Transparent decision-making
        • Continuous validation required
        • Designed for UAE healthcare context
        • Emergency: Call 998 immediately
